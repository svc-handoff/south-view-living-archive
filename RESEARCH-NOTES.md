# Research limits for review

- Child-victim interments follow the supplied project research citing D. L. Henderson, South-View: An African American City of the Dead, page six. The book page and cemetery registers have not been independently checked. These are reported connections, not independently certified burial records.
- Milton Harvey's age is 14 in the linked Atlanta Journal-Constitution gallery. The supplied notes' age of seven was not used.
- Curtis Walker's age is omitted because project sources disagree. Personal hobbies from earlier drafts were removed where they could not be checked.
- Wayne Williams's convictions concerned the adult victims Jimmy Ray Payne and Nathaniel Cater. Investigative attributions are distinguished from convictions.
- Herndon's Atlanta arrival year differs between the New Georgia Encyclopedia (early 1883) and the Atlanta History Center photograph catalog (1882). The biography cites both.
- The massacre chapter's date identifies its beginning on September 22, 1906, rather than asserting a uniform end date across differing accounts.
- Benjamin Elijah Mays's burial at South-View is historical; his remains were later reinterred at Morehouse.
- The foundation's neighborhood profiles document an institutional connection to South-View. For Annie Lou McPheeters, Carolyn Dunbar Yancey, and Horace Mann Bond, those pages are not represented as independent burial-register evidence.
- Existing portrait credits are retained as attributed by the foundation. The asset ledger records where original authorship or publication permission is not documented.
- The linked Mays archive index was checked through the library's public web listing. The former link to a folder of congratulatory notes was removed because it did not support the old school-board description.
- The HTTP source check covers 60 unique links. Some institutional servers return bot-protection errors or time out; the report records these limits. No claim is made that every external link is available from every browser.

No new historical photographs or portraits were added in this revision.
